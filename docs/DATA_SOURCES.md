# Data sources and licensing

The starter pack records a source URL on every question and answer. It uses factual eligibility data from authoritative or first-party references including NASA, NOAA, IUPAC, the United Nations, the US EPA, the CIA World Factbook, the Canadian government, the US Census Bureau, the Folger Shakespeare Library, BFI, the Beatles' official catalogue, the ITF/Olympics, AFI, and first-party game/brand instructions.

The Brillion-authored selection, normalization, aliases, facts, and reviewed recall ordering are released under CC0-1.0. Facts themselves are not copyrightable in many jurisdictions, but downstream reuse must still respect each linked source's terms. No page text or artwork is copied into the product.

The optional REST Countries importer uses the project's published data/API and records its MPL-2.0 attribution plus retrieval time. Future importers must declare their source URL, license, retrieval date, and raw evidence. Do not add indiscriminate HTML scrapers or import a dataset whose commercial reuse terms are unknown.

Question publication requires an exhaustive eligibility check appropriate to the prompt. AI-generated candidates are drafts, not sources and not factual validation.
